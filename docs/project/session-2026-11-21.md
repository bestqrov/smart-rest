## 1. Gulf payments (restaurant-owned gateways)
- Each restaurant enters its own gateway: **Tap, Moyasar, Stripe** (hosted payment pages).
- Secret keys are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM, `src/payments/online/secretBox.ts`, key = `PAYMENT_ENCRYPTION_KEY`).
- Callbacks are never trusted; the server re-fetches the payment from the provider before settling.
- Files: `src/payments/online/{gateways,BillPaymentService,receipt}.ts`, `src/routes/onlinePayment.ts`.
- After payment the order is settled (`paidAt`) and table seats are released.

## 2. Coupons + WhatsApp receipt

## 3. Agency partner program
- Tiers: Silver 25% / Gold 30% / Platinum 35%, recurring monthly.
- Commission is calculated on the amount actually paid, for as long as the restaurant keeps paying; perks are cumulative.
- Agency portal at `/partners`; management at `app/superadmin/agencies`.
- Code: `src/affiliate/{AgencyTiers,AgencyService}.ts`, `src/routes/agencyPartners.ts`.

## 4. Pricing + automatic subscription billing
- Plans: Starter / Pro / Enterprise = **49 / 99 / 199 USD/month** (`lib/gulfPlans.ts`). Starter allows up to 25 tables.
- Features are gated with `src/billing/planAccess.ts`, plus `app/admin/PlanGate.tsx` in the admin UI.
- A daily cron at 04:00 issues invoices (`src/cron/subscriptionBilling.ts`).
- Payment by bank transfer or Tap (Tap stays off until the owner enables it). Public pay page: `/billing/pay/[token]`.
- Dunning reminders at +3, +7 and +14 days, then suspension. A suspended restaurant gets 402 on the admin API only.
- Superadmin pages: `billing/invoices` and `billing/payment-settings` (bank details + Tap key).
